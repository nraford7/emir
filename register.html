Criteria for membership.

Membership rests on three qualities and follows three steps. Together they keep the network what its members value most: senior, trusted and among peers.

What we look for

  1. Seniority

    Members lead at the very top of their organisations and offices, as chairs, board members, CEOs, managing directors, managing partners, directors general, ministers, ambassadors or the regional head of a multinational.

  2. A record of impact

    A demonstrated history of success, influence, operating experience and strategic impact.

  3. Discretion

    Every member agrees to EMIR's confidentiality agreement and house rules, which allow members to speak freely with one another.

How membership happens

  1. A trusted introduction

    A recommendation from a current or former EMIR member, or an invitation from one of EMIR's senior advisors or founding leadership. If you have not yet been introduced, we will seek a recommendation from within the network where there is a fit.

  2. A first gathering

    Candidates join at least one EMIR gathering as our guest.

  3. A personal conversation

    Before membership is considered, every candidate speaks one to one with the EMIR team.
