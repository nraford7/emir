EMIR Private Office.

The Private Office is EMIR's in-house team for special projects and high-stakes mandates.

Complex, ambitious, one-of-a-kind, politically sensitive situations need people who have faced them before. When everyone around you has a stake in the outcome, you need people who have none.

We call these Special Projects. For each one, we draw on the EMIR network to assemble former Chairs, CEOs, Ministers and specialists, and we do the work in confidence.

Some situations are so unusual, sensitive or high-stakes that you don't know who to call. That is when you call us. We bring you the people who have done it before, and they handle it quietly, precisely and completely.
