EMIR Executive Office.

Our work is discreet — and leaves you stronger.

The EMIR Executive Office is our own team for high-stakes mandates. For each one, we draw on the EMIR network to assemble former Chairs, CEOs, Ministers and specialists, and we do the work in confidence. Our Members come first.

Complex, ambiguous, politically sensitive situations need people who have faced them before. When everyone around you has a stake in the outcome, you need people who have none.

Whether the moment calls for a major investment or a turnaround, a global campaign or entry into a new market, the Executive Office is your first port of call. Consultancies send their juniors and a deck. We send the people who used to hire them.
